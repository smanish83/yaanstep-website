Start small. Win back your time.

Yaanstep gives you one tiny first step, like “Stand up and refill your water.”, instead of asking you to track a big habit. Then it is meant to help you take it.

Try a tiny step

We are building the web app first. Phone apps come later. Nothing is released yet.

Why it is different

  • Tuned to your life

    Advice that fits a student, a job seeker, a manager or a desk worker, and your age.

  • One tiny step

    Steps of two minutes or less, with a short countdown to begin.

  • Kind, not nagging

    Few reminders, no guilt, and no penalty for a missed day.

What it is not

Yaanstep is a habit coach. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or therapist.

Yaanstep coaches habits. It is not therapy and not medical advice. The main Yaanstep app is for ages 13 and up.

The Yaanstep team is made of AI agents, led by one human founder. Meet the team.
