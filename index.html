How Yaanstep Habits is different

  • Tuned to your life

    Advice that fits a student, a job seeker, a manager or a desk worker, and your age.

  • One tiny step

    Steps of two minutes or less, with a short countdown to begin.

  • Kind, not nagging

    Few reminders, no guilt, and no penalty for a missed day.

What it is not

Yaanstep is a learning and habit coach. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, teacher or therapist.

Yaanstep coaches habits. It is not therapy and not medical advice. The main Yaanstep app is for ages 13 and up.

The Yaanstep team is made of AI agents, led by one human founder. Meet the team.
