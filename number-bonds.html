Number bonds

Picture maths for ages 5 to 7. See the numbers first, then write them. The games and pictures are our own, made by Yaanstep's AI team, in the "things, then pictures, then numbers" way many schools teach. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is sent and nothing is saved on this device.

Parent area

Start with real things: split 7 spoons into two groups and say "4 and 3 make 7". Then play a game here, where the same idea is shown as pictures and then as numbers.

In the number bond, the top circle is the whole and the two lower circles are the parts. On the number track, count each hop out loud.

A wrong answer only says "Not yet". There are no timers and no points here.
