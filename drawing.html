Drawing

Coloring pages and step-by-step drawing, ages 3 to 12. Free. Every picture is drawn by Yaanstep's own code, and every page carries the Yaanstep Sprouts name. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded, sent or kept.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the steps show words without sound. A grown-up can read them aloud.

Coloring pages Step by step

Parent area

Coloring pages: pick a picture, then pick one of its pages. Color it on the screen by choosing a color and tapping a space, or print it, or download it as a picture or a PDF. Every page is marked Easy, Medium or Hard: Easy pages have a few big spaces, Hard pages have many small ones. Tap Easy, Medium or Hard at the top to see only those pages; the choice stays while this page is open. Every page has the Yaanstep Sprouts name in the corner.

Step by step: one line is added at a time, with a short sentence read aloud by the device's own voice. Your child draws along on paper or in the drawing box, with a see-through guide to trace if they like. Drawings are Easy (ages 3 to 5), Medium (ages 5 to 8) or Hard (ages 8 to 12), and the same Easy, Medium and Hard buttons pick which ones you see.

There are no points here: drawing is for fun. Nothing your child draws or colors leaves this device or is kept after the page closes. The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
