Yaanstep Sprouts

Learning in small steps for ages 3 to 12. Short lessons and games to try with your child. Each one is new every time you play.

Samples only. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not tested with children. Not a replacement for school.

No sign-in. Nothing is sent. No cookies, no trackers, no ads.

The sample lessons on this page are for a parent and child to use together.

Designed with every child in mind, including children who learn in different ways. Comfort settings such as fewer choices, read aloud, calm mode and bigger buttons work across the games, and there is a calm corner for quiet play. How Yaanstep Sprouts supports every child.

Calm play and comfort settings

Every child can play in the way that suits them. You choose by what helps, never by a label, and you do not need to tell us why.

Open the calm corner

The calm corner has slow bubbles to pop, a breathing circle, a picture to color and a "First, then" picture plan. No score, no timer and no way to lose.

Comfort settings are kept only on this device, inside your child's profile. No age is needed to turn them on.

  • Fewer choices: two answers instead of three in most games. One is always right.
  • Read questions aloud: each new question is spoken once with your device's own voice, with a Hear it button.
  • Quiet rewards: a plain tick and words, with nothing moving.
  • Calm mode: nothing moves, and there are no extra sounds or countdowns.
  • Easy reading and an easy-reading font, bigger buttons, strong contrast and break reminders.

Fewer choices does not reach every game yet: the maths chapters, phonogram games, number hunt and the reading page still show all their choices. Not yet tested with children.

How these lessons are built

  • Pictures first. A new idea starts with real things, then a picture, then numbers.
  • Short, with a clear end. In this lesson, a three-year-old spends about 5 minutes on the screen. The rest of the lesson is away from the screen. Nothing plays on by itself.
  • New every time. The questions are made fresh each time you play, and you choose the kind of question.
  • Back to real life. Every lesson ends with one small job to do with real things.
