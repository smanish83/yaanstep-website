Rhymes and letter teams

Listening games and rhymes, best played by a parent and child together. The rhymes are our own words. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. Pictures are simple original drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing your child says is recorded: no microphone, no camera. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A parent can read the question aloud, or try another browser.
