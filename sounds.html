Sounds and beats

Two listening games, best played by a parent and child together. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. Pictures are simple original drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent: no microphone, no camera. Nothing is saved on this device.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A parent can read the question aloud, or try another browser.

Parent area

A computer voice cannot say a sound on its own well, so the games say the letter with an example word, for example "s, as in sock". Say the sound yourself too, and clap the beats together.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
