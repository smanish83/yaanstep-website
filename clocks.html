Clocks and time

Learn to tell the time, then play. Ages 5 to 9. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent. Only points are kept on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the questions aloud.

Parent area

Talk about time through the day: "It is 7 o'clock, time for breakfast." A real clock with hands on the wall helps a lot.

A wrong answer only says what happened, kindly, and offers a short moving lesson with a different time. Nothing is timed. The kitchen timer is pretend: once it is set right, it runs down in a few seconds, and it rings with a picture, not a sound.

Time around the world uses this device's own clock and time zone, so the times are the real times right now. It fits your child's age from the profile: day or night up to age 5, reading the times from age 6, and how many hours ahead from age 8. Set the kitchen timer fits the age too: whole minutes up to 10 up to age 5, minutes and seconds for ages 6 and 7, and "half an hour" or "a quarter of an hour" from age 8. With no profile, both are set in the middle.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well. More clock practice is in Maths.
