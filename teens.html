          What the planner does

          • Planner book: one page for each day with your events, a to-do list and a note.
          • Countdown: every event shows how long until it starts, like "in 2 days 4 hours".
          • Knows the kind of event: "Maths test" is a test in maths, "Sam's party" is an outing.
          • Test help: it asks how you want to prepare and which topics feel hard, then spreads short study blocks across the days left.
          • Repeats: classes and practice can repeat every day or on chosen days of the week.

          Coming next: week and month calendar views, outfit ideas and checklists for outings, a habit builder, and short daily suggestions like a walk or a phone-free hour.
