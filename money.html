Money and coins

Learn about money, then play. Ages 5 to 9. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. It uses US dollars and cents. The coins are simple drawings with their value written on them, not pictures of real coins. No sign-in, no ads, no real money. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the questions aloud.

Parent area

Play shop at home with real coins: put prices on toys, pay and give change. Let your child count the coins, biggest first.

A wrong answer only says what happened, kindly, and offers a short moving lesson with a different example. There are no timers.
