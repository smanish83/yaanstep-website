Grammar: how sentences work

Grammar for ages 4 to 12, best with a grown-up. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Every lesson and sentence is our own. No sign-in, no ads. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the lessons show words without sound. A grown-up can read them aloud.

Parent area

Each topic has a short lesson, one part at a time, where words are colored by the job they do, and practice. Practice starts at your child's age, gets harder after two right answers in a row and easier after a miss. A wrong tap explains kindly and offers a short lesson. There are no timers.

"Whole sentence or a piece?" helps children spot sentence fragments: a whole sentence says who or what, and what happens.

Games: "Mixed-up words" practises words that sound or look alike (then and than, their, there and they're, your and you're, its and it's, and more). "Fill in the blanks" has gap sentences for every topic, and each topic's practice uses them too. "Punctuation detective" asks your child to tap the gap where a mark goes, and to see and hear how a mark changes what a sentence means.
