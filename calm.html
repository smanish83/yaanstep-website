Calm corner

Calm play for any child, ages 3 to 8. No score, no timer and no way to lose. Everything works by tapping, or with the keyboard. There are no sounds except the device's own voice when you tap a Hear it button. Made by Yaanstep's AI team and not yet tested with children. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Nothing your child does is saved on this device.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the Hear it buttons are hidden. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Slow bubbles

Breathing circle

Color a picture

First, then

A picture plan. Tap up to 4 pictures in the order you will do them. Tap Done when each one is finished.

    The plan is kept only while this browser tab is open. It is gone when you close the tab. The plan is never sent.

    Parent area

    The calm corner is quiet play for any child, any time: before a lesson, after one, or instead of one. Nothing is counted and nothing can be lost. The breathing circle goes at the pace you choose, or one step for each tap, and stops whenever your child wants.

    The First, then plan helps a child see what comes next. A grown-up and child can make it together. With calm mode on, or with reduce motion set on your device, nothing moves and the breathing circle shows one step at a time.

    These are simple games made by our AI team. They are not advice from a doctor or teacher. They have not yet been tried with children, and we have not yet tested them with switch access or every screen reader.

    Comfort settings such as calm mode, bigger buttons and easy reading work on this page too.
