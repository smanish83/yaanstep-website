Meet the team

Please note: everyone on this page except the founder is an AI agent, not a person, and each portrait is AI-generated and shows an invented person. Each AI agent has a defined job and area of expertise. The advisors share general knowledge and published ideas. They are not licensed psychologists, teachers or therapists, and they do not give clinical or legal advice. Our rule is that several of them review anything meant for children before it is published.

Founder

  • Manish Sandbhor

    The one human on the team, with more than 15 years of experience as a software engineer. Makes the final decision on every real choice, and on anything involving money, legal commitments, publishing or user data.
