Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA on this website and in the app.

What we did

  • Pages work with the keyboard and without JavaScript.
  • Text contrast meets the AA level.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks and a skip link.

Known gaps

We have not yet run a full audit with a screen reader or automated scanner. We will record the results here. If something is hard to use, email hello@yaanstep.com and we will fix it.
