Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA on this website and in the app.

What we did

  • Pages work with the keyboard and without JavaScript.
  • Text contrast is meant to meet the AA level, and the automated scan checks it.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks and a skip link.

Yaanstep Sprouts for every child

We design Yaanstep Sprouts so that every child can play and learn in the way that suits them. You choose settings by what helps your child, never by a label, and we never ask why. We follow published guidance: WCAG 2.2, the W3C work on making content usable for people who think and learn in different ways, guidance on readable type and spacing, and Universal Design for Learning.

What exists now

  • Comfort settings, kept only on this device. Turn on any of them from the comfort settings on Yaanstep Sprouts or from your child's profile. No age is needed.
    • Easy reading: bigger text with more space, on a cream background.
    • Easy-reading font: plain, wide letters from a font already on your device. Nothing is downloaded.
    • Fewer choices: two answers instead of three in most games. One answer is always right.
    • Read questions aloud: each new question is spoken once with your device's own voice, and a Hear it button says it again.
    • Quiet rewards: a plain tick and words, with nothing moving.
    • Calm mode: nothing moves, and there are no extra sounds or countdowns.
    • Bigger buttons (at least 64 pixels), strong contrast and break reminders.
  • A calm corner: slow bubbles, a breathing circle, a picture to color and a "First, then" picture plan. No score, no timer and nothing to lose. The plan is kept only while the browser tab is open.
  • Slow things to watch: a turning fan, soft lights, a lava lamp, fish, falling snow, sea waves, a pinwheel and balloons. Each moves slowly only after a tap, with no sound and no flashing, and stops on Stop.
  • Read along: in Story time, the Story library, Sentences and the Reading common words the voice can read each page, and a marker lights up each word as it is read. Every spoken line is also on screen.
  • Kind mistakes: a wrong answer gets a hint and another try, never a buzzer. After a miss, a short moving-picture lesson is one tap away.
  • Nothing rushed: the games have no time limits. With calm mode, or with reduce motion on your device, nothing moves.
  • Private: settings stay on this device. Nothing is sent, and an automated test checks that no request carries them.

What does not exist yet

  • Fewer choices does not reach every game yet. The maths chapters, phonogram games, number hunt and the reading page still show all their choices.
  • Read questions aloud is checked by our tests on the number bonds, tens and ones, fractions and bar model pages, where it is new. Most other games already speak each question. The bar model word problem itself is not read out yet, only each step.
  • Rewards can be full or quiet. There is no setting yet to turn rewards off.
  • We have not tested with switch access or with every screen reader.
  • Nothing here has been tried with children yet. We will say here when it has.

Yaanstep Sprouts is a set of learning games. It is not a medical or educational assessment, and it makes no health claims. If something would help your child, email hello@yaanstep.com.

Known gaps

An automated accessibility scan runs on every page before each release. We have not yet tested with a screen reader, and will record the results here when we do. If something is hard to use, email hello@yaanstep.com and we will fix it.

Yaanstep coaches habits. It is not therapy and not medical advice. The main Yaanstep app is for ages 13 and up.
