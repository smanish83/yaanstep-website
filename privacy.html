Privacy

DRAFT. This text was written by the Yaanstep AI team. No lawyer has reviewed it, and it is not legal advice. It describes the app as planned; the app is not released yet. We will update this page to match what the app really does before release.

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We do not see your Google password.
  • We keep your own tasks, schedule and progress in your own space so you can use the app on your phone and on the web.
  • Analytics are optional. If you turn them on, we collect only: a random ID, an age group, a profile type, task categories and whether steps were started. Never your name, email, birth date or the words you type.
  • For ages 13 to 17, analytics are off unless you turn them on.
  • You can export or delete your data and your account at any time.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. If you email us to join the test, we keep your email address on a private list and delete it when you ask.

What we are still deciding

How long we keep data after you delete your account, and where it is stored, will be written here once decided.
