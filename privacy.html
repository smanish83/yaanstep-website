Privacy

DRAFT. This text was written by the Yaanstep AI team. No lawyer has reviewed it, and it is not legal advice. It describes the app as planned; the app is not released yet. We will update this page to match what the app really does before release.

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We do not see your Google password.
  • We keep your own tasks, schedule and progress in your own space so you can use the app on your phone and on the web.
  • Analytics are optional. If you turn them on, we collect only: a random ID, an age group, a profile type, task categories and whether steps were started. Never your name, email, birth date or the words you type.
  • For ages 13 to 17, analytics are off unless you turn them on.
  • You can export or delete your data and your account at any time.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. It remembers your theme choice, voice settings and progress in the learning games, only in your browser. If you pick your child's age on the learning pages, it also keeps that age, a color and an optional nickname in your browser, so the right games show. Nothing is kept until you pick an age, and "Remove profile" deletes it. The avatar is a drawing made by the page; there is no photo upload. If you email us to join the test, we keep your email address on a private list and delete it when you ask.

Yaanstep Teens planner (early preview)

The planner keeps your events, to-dos, notes and study plans only in this browser, under one storage entry. Nothing is sent to Yaanstep or anyone else, and there is no sign-in. It does not ask for your name, school, birthday or location. "Delete everything" on the planner page removes it all, and so does clearing your browser data. If we add an optional backup with sign-in later, we will update this page first and, for younger users, ask a parent where the law requires it.

What we are still deciding

How long we keep data after you delete your account, and where it is stored, will be written here once decided.
