How it works

  1. Pick your profile

    Student, job seeker, manager or desk worker. You can change it any time.

  2. Get a tiny step

    Instead of "move more", you get "Stand up and refill your water." Each step takes two minutes or less.

  3. Start in seconds

    Tap Start Now. A 10-second countdown begins, then the app gets out of the way.
