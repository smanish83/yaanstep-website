How it works

  1. Pick your profile

    Student, job seeker, manager or desk worker. You can change it any time.

  2. Get a tiny step

    Instead of "work out", you get "put on your shoes". Each step takes two minutes or less.

  3. Start in 60 seconds

    Tap Start Now. A short countdown begins, then the app gets out of the way.
