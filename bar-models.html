Bar models

Maths word problems you can see. Draw a bar for each person or thing, change the bars as you read, then put a question mark where the answer goes. The problems are our own, written by Yaanstep's AI team, using the bar model method many schools teach. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. No sign-in, no ads. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

How a bar model works

  1. Read the problem.
  2. Find who and what.
  3. Draw a bar for each one.
  4. Read again, one sentence at a time, and change the bars to match.
  5. Put the question mark where the answer goes.
  6. Work it out.
  7. Say the answer in a full sentence.

Pick an age group:

Parent area

Each set has 5 short word problems, made fresh every time. Let your child read each sentence and watch the bars change. Ask "What do the bars show now?" before you tap on.

Ages 5 to 6 put two parts together or find the part that is left. Ages 7 to 8 also compare: how many more. Ages 9 to 10 add "times as many" and two-step problems. Ages 11 to 12 share a total into equal units and solve three-step problems.

A wrong answer only says "Not yet" and asks your child to look at the bars again. There are no timers here.
