How these lessons are built

  • Pictures first. A new idea starts with real things, then a picture, then numbers.
  • Short, with a clear end. A lesson for a three-year-old is about 20 minutes, and half of it happens away from the screen. Nothing plays on by itself.
  • New every time. The questions are made fresh each time you play, and you choose the kind of question.
  • Back to real life. Every lesson ends with one small job to do with real things.
