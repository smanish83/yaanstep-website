Learning in small steps

We are designing a maths and reading program for children aged 3 to 13. It is not available yet. Here are two short sample lessons you can try with your child.

Please read first. These are samples only. They were designed by Yaanstep's AI team and have not been tested with children. They do not replace school or a teacher.

A parent or caregiver should sit with the child. There is no sign-in. Nothing your child does here is saved or sent anywhere. This page sets no cookies and uses no trackers.

Pick a sample lesson

  • Age 3, with a parent · about 8 minutes

    Same and different

    Match socks, spot the odd one out and sort blocks by color. Then a book and a small job away from the screen.

  • Age 6 · about 10 minutes

    Picture the problem

    Turn a word problem into a bar picture, then find the missing number.

What the two sample lessons cover

LessonOn the screenAway from the screen

Picture the problem (age 6)See how a bar picture shows a word problem, then solve four problems with barsCount two groups of things at home, draw a bar for each and find the total

How these lessons are built

  • Pictures first. A new idea starts with real things, then a picture, then numbers.
  • Short, with a clear end. A lesson for a three-year-old is about 20 minutes, and half of it happens away from the screen. Nothing plays on by itself.
  • A small check, not a test. Each lesson ends with a few questions so you can see what stuck. For young children, the parent simply notes how it went.
  • Back to real life. Every lesson ends with one small job to do with real things.

The full program is still being designed. If it is built, a parent will hold the account and a child will never sign in.

Yaanstep coaches habits. It is not therapy and not medical advice. The Yaanstep app is for ages 13 and up. The sample lessons on this page are for a parent and child to use together.
