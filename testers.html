Join the testers

We are building the web app first, so the first test will be in your web browser. The Android phone app comes later, and its closed test needs at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row.

How it works

  1. Email us and say whether you would test in a web browser, on an Android phone, or both.
  2. We add you to the test list and send an invite.
  3. Open the link in the invite. For the phone test, accept it and install the app.

Email to join

We ask only for your email address and which test you want to join. We keep the list in one private place and delete your entry on request. The app is not ready yet; we will email when the first build is.

Yaanstep coaches habits. It is not therapy and not medical advice. The main Yaanstep app is for ages 13 and up.
