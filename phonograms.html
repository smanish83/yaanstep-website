Phonogram games

Moving picture lessons and games about phonograms, best played by a parent and child together. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. Pictures are simple original drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent: no microphone, no camera. Nothing is saved on this device.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A parent can read the question aloud, or try another browser.

A phonogram is a letter, or a team of letters, that writes one sound. Start with the lesson, then play:

Parent area

A computer voice cannot say a sound on its own well, so each phonogram is said with an example word, for example "sh, as in shoe". Say the sound yourself too, and say it together.

The words start with one letter for each sound, then letter teams like sh and ck, then vowel teams like ee, oa and ar. Some phonograms write more than one sound in other words; these games use the most common sound.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well. Letter teams are also in Rhymes and letter teams.
