Find the difference

Looking games: odd one out and spot the differences, ages 3 to 8. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent. Only points are kept on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Parent area

These games are about looking carefully and comparing. They start from the age in your child's profile, and in the middle when no age is picked. Then they follow how your child plays: a puzzle solved with no missed taps makes the next one a little trickier (more pictures, smaller changes), and a few missed taps or a clue makes the next one easier. This is kept only while the page is open. There are no timers and no losing. After a quiet pause, a soft outline shows one place to look.

Talk while you play: "What is the same? What changed? How did you know?" Try it away from the screen too, with spoons, socks or toys.

With "Fewer choices" turned on in the child profile, Odd one out shows only 3 pictures at a time. With "Calm mode" or reduced motion, the clue is a still outline.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
