Try a tiny step

This is a small demo of the Yaanstep idea. Pick the profile closest to your life, get a first step that takes two minutes or less, and press Start Now.

The app starts with four profiles: student, job seeker, manager and desk worker. The extra ones here show where it could go and may change.

It runs in your browser only. Nothing you pick is saved or sent anywhere. The real app is still in development.

Why so small?

A big goal is easy to put off. A step you can finish in under two minutes is hard to argue with. Once you have started, carrying on is often easier than you expect. If you stop after the tiny step, that still counts.
