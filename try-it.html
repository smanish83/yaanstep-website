Try a tiny step

This is a small demo of the Yaanstep idea. Pick the profile closest to your life, get a first step that takes two minutes or less, and press Start Now.

The steps below are drafts written from general habit ideas. They have not been tested with real users yet.

It runs in your browser only. Nothing you pick is saved or sent anywhere. The real app is still in development.

Examples of tiny steps

ProfileExample of a tiny first step
StudentOpen your notebook or laptop to today's page.
Job seekerSit at your work spot and open your resume file.
Desk jobWrite your one top task for today on a sticky note.
ManagerWrite down one person on your team who deserves a thank-you.
Teen (13 to 17)Flip your phone over for 2 minutes. Just 2.

Why so small?

A big goal is easy to put off. Our idea is that a very small step is easier to begin. We have not tested this yet. If you stop after the tiny step, that still counts.
