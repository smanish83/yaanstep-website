Opposites

Opposites with pictures and words, ages 3 to 12. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent. Only points are kept on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Parent area

Opposites are words for how something is, like hot or cold, big or small. Words for what a thing is (apple, dog) or what it is made of (plastic, wood) have no opposite. Now and then a game asks about one of these, and the right answer is "No opposite!". The picture games suit ages 3 to 6. Word opposites reads each word aloud and suits ages 5 and up, with harder words as your child grows. Make the opposite, for ages 7 and up, shows how un, dis, in and im turn a word into its opposite. Each game fits the age in your child's profile, and sits in the middle when no age is picked. There are no timers and no losing. A wrong tap says what was tapped, kindly, and offers a short moving lesson.

Talk while you play: "Is it a way something can be? Then what is the other way?"

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
