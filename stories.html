Story time

Short picture stories to read with a grown-up. The stories are our own words, written by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school or for real books. Pictures are simple original drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent: no microphone, no camera. Nothing is saved on this device.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the stories show words without sound. A grown-up can read them aloud, or try another browser.

Parent area

Read each page together. Tap "Read this page to me" if you want the voice to read it. Then ask the question on the page and let your child answer in their own words. Some questions ask what happened, and some ask what your child thinks. Any answer is a good start, and nothing is recorded.

At the end, tap the words from the story to hear them again. Real paper books are great too.

In "Half a bun", Sam shares food. You may like to talk about asking a grown-up first before sharing food.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
