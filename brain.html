Brain games

Puzzles and games for thinking, ages 3 to 8. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Parent area

These are puzzles and games for thinking: remembering, spotting patterns, sorting, shapes and looking carefully. Each game fits the age in your child's profile, and sits in the middle when no age is picked. There are no timers and no losing. A wrong tap says what was tapped, kindly, and offers a short moving lesson.

Talk while you play: "What is the same? What is different? How did you know?" Play away from the screen too, with cups and small toys.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
