Big and small

Big and small, more and fewer, ages 3 to 8. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. We count visits and which games are used, never who you are. Points are kept only on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Parent area

These games are about comparing: bigger and smaller, more and fewer, and which number is bigger. Each game fits the age in your child's profile, and sits in the middle when no age is picked. After a few right answers in a row the next ones get a little closer, and after a miss they get easier again; this is kept only while the page is open. There are no timers and no losing. A wrong tap says what was tapped, kindly, and offers a short moving lesson.

In "Which is smaller?" the pictures are sometimes drawn the same size, or the wrong way round, on purpose: the question is about the real things. Talk about it: "Could a cup fit in a bucket? How many cups would fill it?"

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
