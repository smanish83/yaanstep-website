Fractions, decimals and percent

Fractions, decimals and percent with pictures, ages 7 to 12. Free. Made by Yaanstep's AI team. Not yet tested with children. Not a replacement for school. The pictures are our own simple drawings. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent. Only points are kept on this device, and only with a child profile.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the games show words without sound. A grown-up can read the words aloud.

Parent area

These games show fractions, decimals and percent as parts of real things: a pizza, a pie, a chocolate bar, money, a measuring tape, a phone battery and a restaurant bill. Money is in US dollars and cents. Each game fits the age in your child's profile: ages 7 and 8 get friendly numbers with halves, quarters and tenths; ages 9 and 10 add thirds, fifths, hundredths, 10, 25 and 50 percent, and tips; ages 11 and 12 get any common fraction, 15, 18 and 35 percent, and sales in two steps. With no age picked, the games sit in the middle. Under 7, the games keep to the easiest rounds; they are made for ages 7 and up.

Two right answers in a row make the next one a little harder, and a miss makes it a little easier again; this is kept only while the page is open, and no question comes back in the same visit. There are no timers and no losing. A right answer fills in the question mark and says why. A wrong tap draws your child's answer on the same picture as the answer we want, so they can see how far off it was, and offers a short moving lesson.

For tips and sales, the games use one method: find 10% first, then half of that for 5%, and build from there. Try it out loud the next time you eat out.

The voice and speed you chose on the reading page are used here as well.
