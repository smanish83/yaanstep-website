Story library

Complete picture books to read together, for ages 3 to 12.

Free books from open libraries. Each book was written and drawn by the people named in its credits and shared under an open licence (CC BY) by StoryWeaver, African Storybook or Book Dash. Yaanstep's AI team picked and checked them for young children, but please look through a book before reading it with your child. Pictures are resized; the words are as the authors wrote them. No sign-in, no ads. Nothing is recorded or sent.

No built-in voice was found on this device, so the books show words without sound. A grown-up can read them aloud, or try another browser.
